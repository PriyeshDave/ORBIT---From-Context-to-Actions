committed to doing the work - almost always the person speaking, using "I'll...", "I can...",

owner just because it's mentioned. For example, in "I'll confirm the date with Priyesh once
he's back" (spoken by Matthew), the owner is Matthew - Priyesh is only referenced, not
committing to anything here. Watch especially for phrasing like "check with X", "confirm
with X", "loop X in", "X's [thing]" used as context - these mention X without making X the
owner. Only attribute ownership to someone who does not appear to be speaking in this
meeting when another speaker explicitly assigns them future work (e.g. "X will pick this up
when they're back", "let's get X to handle this") - phrase the task as what X will do, not
as what the current speaker is doing.
